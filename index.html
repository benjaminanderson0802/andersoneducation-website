Our mission

Better opportunities for people who already know how to teach.

Educators spend years developing the ability to teach, communicate, encourage, and connect with students.

Anderson Education exists to help educators put those skills to work in a flexible way that fits around their lives.

We believe experienced educators should have better opportunities to earn from the work they already know how to do.
Why we're building this

Education takes more than the hours on the clock.

Educators often spend significant time preparing lessons, grading, communicating with students and parents, and handling work outside of their scheduled hours.

The responsibility is enormous, but the value placed on that experience does not always reflect it.

We want to create another option for educators who want to earn additional income without taking on another traditional job.
