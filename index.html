Bridging education across borders.

Anderson Education connects great English educators with students around the world who may not otherwise have access to native English-speaking teachers.

Our Mission

Great educators shouldn't be limited by geography.

There are students around the world who want to learn English from native English speakers, but don't always have access to the right teachers.

At the same time, there are experienced educators with the knowledge, patience, and passion to teach who may be looking for flexible opportunities to continue doing what they love.

Anderson Education exists to bring these two groups together.

The Connection

Two worlds. One classroom.

Technology makes it possible for a teacher in one part of the world to sit down and teach a student thousands of miles away. We believe that connection can create opportunities for both.
